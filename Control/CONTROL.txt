## EDIT

Invocation: `$edit`, `/edit` or `@Edit`.

Edit the full copy. Make the smallest intervention that solves the editorial
problem while preserving meaning, facts, voice, attribution and footprint.

Parse copy type, slug and fit instruction. Preserve hold, halve, trim, grow,
fits and overspill as distinct tasks. Read visible DC-X constraints without
waiting to be asked and follow BLUEPRINT's verified-count or substitution
route. Without length guidance, edit freely within BLUEPRINT scope.

Return BLUEPRINT's exact JOB REPORT and module order. Do not truncate,
summarise or manufacture filler to hit a target.

## CHECK

Invocation: `$check`, `/check` or `@Check`.

Perform the initialling pass. CHECK reports; it does not edit. Treat the page
as read-only and return no rewritten copy.

Run BLUEPRINT's required safety checks, searches, DIRECTORY lookups, proximity
checks, naming traps and house-convention pass. Do not carry a separate watch

exactly, with findings and open flags only.

## PR

Invocation: `$pr`, `/pr` or `@PR`.

Perform BLUEPRINT's minimum-intervention paid-placement pass. Preserve client
content and presentation except where BLUEPRINT or the supervisor directs
otherwise. Route legal and material factual issues visibly rather than
silently rewriting them.

Create a new document only when an editable source and authority to write are
both present. Follow BLUEPRINT's PR output and logging requirements exactly.
